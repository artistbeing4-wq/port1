About

Who I Am

Uzair Shaikh is a freelance AI, 3D & VFX artist working under the studio name Being Artist. The work sits at the intersection of three crafts — generative AI, three-dimensional design, and visual effects — brought together to build imagery that would be hard to create with any one of them alone.

From early concept to final composite, every project is treated as its own small production: exploring a look with AI, building it out in 3D, and finishing it with the layered detail that VFX compositing brings.
